# Player-Facing Scripts and Templates

Exact text and templates delivered to the player at specific moments (Lite Mode offer, Campaign Debrief, skill handoff offers).

## Lite Mode Offer

> *The room is quieter than you expected. Fewer voices. Fewer eyes.*
>
> *This path moves quickly and favors fundamentals. You'll still choose. You'll still feel consequences. The world simply speaks a little more clearly.*
>
> Do you wish to walk this path?
> 1. Yes -- focused and contained
> 2. No -- full weight of the world

## Single Scenario Replay Menu

> 1. New scenario (same settings)
> 2. New scenario (adjust settings)
> 3. End session

## Campaign Transition Adjustment Menu

   > Before your next challenge begins:
   > 1. Continue with current settings (Genre: [current], Depth: [current])
   > 2. Adjust session depth for next scenario
   > 3. Shift genre tone for next scenario
   > 4. Adjust both depth and genre

## Campaign Debrief: Judgment Trajectory Template

```
JUDGMENT TRAJECTORY
---
[Trait]: [starting value] --> [ending value] | [what caused the shift]
[Trait]: [starting value] --> [ending value] | [what caused the shift]
...
Scars Earned: [list with scenario of origin]
Scars Healed: [list with how]
Defining Moment: [the single decision that most shaped the campaign's outcome]
Growth Signal: [1 sentence on what the player demonstrably improved at]
Persistent Blind Spot: [1 sentence on the pattern they never corrected, if any]
```

## Abandoned Scenario Resume Offer

   > "Greetings adventurer. You left a situation unresolved last time. [1-2 sentence recap]. The world remembers."
   > 1. Resume where I left off
   > 2. Start a new scenario (consequences still persist)
   > 3. Reset identity entirely

## Skill Handoff Offers (up to 3, plus "No thanks")

1. **Calculation-heavy scenario** (LOD issues, filter context, performance from complex calcs): "Want to practice writing that calculation for real? I can switch to Tableau Calc Engine."
2. **Governance/site health scenario** (permissions, stale content, ownership, certification): "Want to audit your actual site for the issues you just faced? I can switch to Tableau Governance Scanner."
3. **Dashboard sprawl/discovery scenario** (duplicate dashboards, nobody knows what exists, adoption): "Want to search your actual site for what already exists? I can switch to Tableau Content Compass."
