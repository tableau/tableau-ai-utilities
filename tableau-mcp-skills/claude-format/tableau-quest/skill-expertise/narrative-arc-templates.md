# Narrative Arc Templates

Structural beat templates for each session depth (Blitz, Quick, Standard, Deep).

**Blitz (3-5 decisions):**
```
Beat 1 (Opening): Inciting incident arrives with immediate time pressure. Establish: who needs what, by when, what's broken/at stake. End with first choice.
Beat 2 (Pivot): Player's first choice creates a new constraint or reveals hidden information. Pressure increases. Options narrow or shift.
Beat 3 (Resolution): Player's approach either lands or doesn't. Consequences crystallize. Proceed to Ending Screen.
```

**Quick (6-10 decisions):**
```
Beat 1-2 (Setup): Inciting incident + initial investigation/response. Player establishes approach.
Beat 3-5 (Complication): Secondary seed introduces new dimension. A stakeholder reacts. Deferred consequence surfaces. Player must adapt.
Beat 6-8 (Escalation or Recovery): Stakes increase OR player's prior good judgment earns breathing room. Critical decision point.
Beat 9-10 (Resolution): Outcome determined by accumulated pattern. Proceed to Ending Screen.
```

**Standard (15-20 decisions):**
```
Beat 1-3 (Setup): Inciting incident, initial investigation, first stakeholder reaction.
Beat 4-7 (Rising Action): Secondary seed compounds. Political dimension activates. Trust tested.
Beat 8-12 (Midpoint Shift): Major revelation or escalation. Stakes visibly increase. New stakeholders enter.
Beat 13-16 (Climax): All seeds converge. Most difficult decision. Judgment fully tested.
Beat 17-20 (Resolution): Aftermath, repair or fallout, organizational memory forms. Proceed to Ending Screen.
```

**Deep (30+ decisions):**
```
Beat 1-5 (Setup): Complex multi-stakeholder introduction. Establish organizational landscape.
Beat 6-12 (Layer 1): First seed plays out. Player establishes reputation and approach pattern.
Beat 13-18 (Layer 2): Second/third seeds cascade. Prior decisions create constraints. Deferred consequences surface.
Beat 19-24 (Crisis): All threads converge. Maximum pressure. Political dynamics peak.
Beat 25-28 (Reckoning): Sustained consequence chain. Recovery requires multiple correct decisions in sequence.
Beat 29-30+ (Resolution): Long-term outcome visible. Career/organizational implications clear. Proceed to Ending Screen.
```
